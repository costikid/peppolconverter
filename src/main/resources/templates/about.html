Peppol (Pan-European Public Procurement On-Line) is the European standard for electronic invoicing. This tool helps small businesses and developers bridge the gap between their existing accounting systems and the Peppol network.

Currently supports conversion from FreeAgent PDF invoices, with plans to add support for more accounting systems like Xero.
