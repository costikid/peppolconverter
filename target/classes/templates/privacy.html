Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

Data Storage

Uploaded PDF files are stored temporarily in your browser session and are not persisted on our servers. Once your session expires or you close your browser, the data is deleted.

What We Process

We only extract the invoice data necessary for conversion to Peppol format: invoice number, dates, amounts, line items, and party details.

Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking services. This is a self-hosted tool.

Cookies

We use session cookies to maintain your conversion state during the upload process. No persistent tracking cookies are used.
