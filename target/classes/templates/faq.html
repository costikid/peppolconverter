A buyer endpoint is the Peppol participant identifier for the recipient of your invoice (e.g. 0192:123456789). It is required for routing the invoice through the Peppol network. If you have the buyer's Peppol ID, enter it during upload. If not, you can look it up in your country's Peppol directory or ask your buyer.

The maximum file size is 10MB and a maximum of 10 pages. FreeAgent invoices are typically well under 1MB.
